How to keep your iPhone screen awake

Stop your iPhone or iPad screen from turning off — without changing Auto-Lock and without installing any app. It runs right in Safari, free.

Reading a recipe, following sheet music, scanning a QR menu, or watching a doc on your phone? iOS dims and locks the screen after your Auto-Lock timeout. You could dig into Settings every time — or just open ScreenWakeUp, which uses the Screen Wake Lock API (iOS 16.4+) to hold the screen on while the tab is open.

Quick version: open screenwakeup.com in Safari → tap Keep Screen Awake → keep the tab visible. Done.

Keep your iPhone screen on in 3 steps

  1. Open screenwakeup.com in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Choose a duration — 30 min, 1h, 2h, 4h, or ∞.
  3. Tap Keep Screen Awake and keep the Safari tab open and on screen.

Tip: add it to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app.

Why not just change Auto-Lock?

You can set Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock > Never — but then your screen never locks, draining the battery and leaving the phone unlocked all day. ScreenWakeUp keeps the screen on only while you need it, then everything goes back to normal the moment you close the tab. No settings to remember to undo.

What you need

  • iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or later for the native Wake Lock API in Safari.
  • The browser tab must stay open and visible — if you switch apps or lock the phone, iOS releases the lock and re-acquires it when you return.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, identically — Safari or Chrome on iPadOS. Great for recipes, presentations and kiosk-style displays.

Will it drain my battery?

Keeping any screen on uses power, but only while the tab is active. Close it and normal Auto-Lock resumes immediately, so it's far safer than setting Auto-Lock to Never.
