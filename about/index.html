About us

We built the tool we needed ourselves

ScreenWakeUp was born out of frustration. During a long file upload, the screen locked, the transfer was interrupted, and we had to start over. We looked for a simple online tool — and found nothing that just worked without requiring a download, an account, or a Chrome extension.

So we built one. One page. One button. No install, no account, no paywall. That's it.

What is ScreenWakeUp?

ScreenWakeUp is a free, browser-based tool that prevents your screen from sleeping or locking. It uses the Screen Wake Lock API — a modern web standard built into Chrome, Edge, and Safari — to request your operating system to keep the display on.

It works on Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android, Linux, and Chromebook. No download, no extension, no account. Just open the page and click one button.

Who uses it?

Remote workers who need to stay active in Teams or Slack. Teachers giving presentations. Students following online lectures. Cooks reading recipes hands-free. Developers monitoring dashboards. Anyone who needs their screen to stay on without touching system settings.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free with no time limits. The core tool — wake lock, timer, 5 languages, dark/light mode — will always be free. We offer an optional Pro plan for users who want an ad-free experience and extra features like longer timers, email alerts, and Pomodoro mode.

Privacy & tracking

We don't collect personal data. We use Google Analytics to count visitors (anonymized). We don't store anything you type, and nothing leaves your browser. Read our full Privacy Policy.
