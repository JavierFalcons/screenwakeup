Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What we collect

ScreenWakeUp collects minimal data to operate the site:

  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to count page visits, session duration, and general geographic region (country-level only). IP addresses are anonymized. We do not use advertising cookies.
  • Feedback form: If you submit a message via the feedback form, we receive your name (optional) and message via FormSubmit.co. This data is sent to our email and not stored by us beyond normal email retention.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not track keystrokes, mouse movements, or screen activity.
  • We do not create user accounts or store passwords.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize your data with third parties.
  • The wake lock feature runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers.

3. Cookies

We use one first-party cookie to remember your language preference (ka-lang). Google Analytics sets its own cookies for session tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics 4 — visitor analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • FormSubmit.co — form submission relay. See their Privacy Policy.
  • Ko-fi — voluntary donations. We do not receive payment details.
  • Lemon Squeezy — Pro plan payments. Payment is handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy. We receive only a confirmation of purchase.

5. Children

ScreenWakeUp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your rights

You can request deletion of any data you've submitted (e.g. feedback messages) by emailing us at rjfalcon@live.com. Since we collect no personal accounts, there is typically nothing to delete.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page reflects the last update. Continued use of ScreenWakeUp after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: rjfalcon@live.com
