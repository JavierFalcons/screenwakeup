Why does Teams show me as Away?

Teams reads two signals: your system idle time (roughly 5 minutes without real input) and your machine state — locked, asleep, or screensaver means instant Away. ScreenWakeUp fixes the second signal completely: while it runs, your screen never locks or sleeps. The first signal is out of reach for any website: browsers deliberately prevent web pages from generating real system input, so JavaScript "mouse simulators" only move a virtual pointer inside their own page. If a site claims its fake keystrokes reset your system idle timer, it's overpromising.

When does Teams go idle?

Microsoft Teams goes idle — and shows you as Away — after about 5 minutes without keyboard or mouse input, and immediately when your computer locks, sleeps, or starts the screensaver. That instant trigger is the one most people hit without realizing it: you step away, Windows locks the screen after a few minutes, and Teams flips to Away even if you're back at your desk reading. Stop the lock and you stop the most common cause of going idle.
