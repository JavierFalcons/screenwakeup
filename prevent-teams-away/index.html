How to prevent the Teams Away status

Keep your Microsoft Teams status green and Active — without moving your mouse, installing anything, or asking IT for admin rights.

If Teams keeps flipping you to Away the moment you step away to read, take a call, or focus on another window, you're not alone. Teams reads your operating system's idle timer: after about 5 minutes with no mouse or keyboard input — or whenever your screen locks or sleeps — it switches your presence to Away. Leaving the Teams window open does nothing, because the system still sees no activity.

The fix in one line: keep the system idle timer reset (and the screen awake) from a browser tab. That's exactly what ScreenWakeUp does — free, in your browser, with no install.

Keep Teams Active in 3 steps

  1. Open the tool. Go to screenwakeup.com in Chrome or Edge, in a separate tab.
  2. Turn on anti-idle. In the "Stay Active in Teams / Slack / Zoom" section, enable both Simulate mouse movement and Send silent F15 key.
  3. Click Keep Screen Awake. Leave the tab open and visible. Your status stays green for as long as the tab runs.

Why does Teams show me as Away?

Teams calculates presence from two signals: your system idle time and your screen state. A long read, a phone call, or a screen that dims for power-saving all look identical to "the user left." ScreenWakeUp addresses both at once — it holds a screen wake lock and sends a silent F15 keystroke plus a tiny pointer movement every 55 seconds, which resets the idle counter without disturbing anything you're doing.

Why not a desktop "mouse jiggler" app?

  • No install, no admin rights — works on locked-down corporate laptops where you can't install software.
  • Nothing stored on your machine — it runs in a tab and disappears when you close it.
  • Cross-platform — same tool on Windows, macOS and Chromebook.
  • Free, no account — and it doubles as a screen-awake tool, Pomodoro timer and fullscreen clock.

Frequently asked questions

Is this against Microsoft Teams' rules?

ScreenWakeUp never touches Teams, your account, or company data — it only stops your screen from sleeping and keeps the OS idle timer from tripping, the same thing that happens when you read a long document. Always follow your own organization's policies.

Does it work without admin rights?

Yes. It's just a web page, so there's nothing to install and no permissions to request — ideal for managed work laptops.

Does it work on Slack and Zoom too?

Yes. The same anti-idle method keeps Slack showing as Active and prevents Zoom from marking you idle. Open the tool, enable the two options, and start the wake lock before your session.
